About Autism Support Hub

Founded on the belief that every autistic individual deserves the right support, at the right time, in the right way.

Who We Are

More than a service — a genuine partnership

Autism Support Hub was founded in 2013 with a simple but powerful mission: to ensure autistic children, teens and young adults receive the structured, proactive and compassionate support they need to thrive across all areas of life.

We combine clinical expertise with genuine understanding, delivering services that work collaboratively with families, schools, employers and support teams to create consistent, sustainable outcomes.

We believe successful transitions don't happen by accident — they are planned, supported and skill-built over time. That philosophy drives everything we do.
