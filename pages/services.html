Our Services

Comprehensive, evidence-informed support across every stage — from early learning through to meaningful employment.

Transition Support

Primary to High School Transition Support

Transitioning from primary to high school can be overwhelming for autistic students. New environments, multiple teachers, changing classrooms, social pressures and increased academic expectations can significantly impact regulation, confidence and participation.

  • Executive functioning and organisation systems
  • Timetable and homework planning
  • Social navigation and peer relationship coaching
  • Self-advocacy skill development
  • Emotional regulation strategies for larger school settings
  • High school orientation preparation
  • Sensory mapping of new environments
  • Transition planning meetings with schools
  • Teacher strategy guides
  • Parent coaching and home support planning

Employment Readiness

School to Work Transition Services

The transition from school to employment is a critical life stage. Many autistic teens and young adults have strong skills but require structured guidance to navigate workplace expectations, communication demands and increasing independence.

  • Career interest and strengths identification
  • Interview preparation and role-play
  • Workplace communication coaching
  • Understanding workplace expectations and routines
  • Emotional regulation in workplace settings
  • Executive functioning support (time management, organisation)
  • Social and professional boundaries
  • Self-advocacy training
  • Workplace behaviour preparation
