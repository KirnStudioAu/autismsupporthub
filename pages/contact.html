Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you NDIS registered?

Yes. Autism Support Hub is a registered NDIS provider. We work with plan-managed and self-managed participants. Contact us to discuss how your plan can support your goals.

Do you offer telehealth services?

Yes. Many of our services are available via telehealth, allowing us to support families across all of Australia regardless of location.

How quickly can we get started?

Following your initial enquiry, we typically schedule a free consultation within 5–7 business days to discuss your needs and begin planning the right support pathway.

Do you work with adults as well as children?

Absolutely. We provide support across the lifespan — from early childhood through to young adulthood and beyond, including employment and workplace services.
